# MoneroKon Governance Framework

## 1. Bodies and precedence

**1.1** The organisation of each MoneroKon conference (an "Edition") is governed by three bodies: the Council (the "Body"), the Organisation Team (the "Orga") and Funds Management (the "Custodian").

**1.2** Where their decisions conflict, the Council prevails over the Organisation Team. The Council is the decision-maker for any matter this framework does not assign elsewhere.

## 2. The Council

**2.1 Role.** The Council is a decision-making Body for MoneroKon. It approves expenditure and all major decisions (clause 6), admits and removes its own members, and appoints and removes members of the Organisation Team and the Fund Signatories.

**2.2 Composition and roll.** The Council consists of at least three persons who are *Previous MoneroKon Volunteers* or *Contributors* to the Monero project. 

- (a) **Previous MoneroKon Volunteer** means a natural person who, before the relevant date of assessment, provided at least 8 hours of unpaid voluntary assistance in connection with the operation of a prior MoneroKon Edition. Such assistance may include, but is not limited to, event setup or teardown, attendee registration, stagehand duties, staffing the coffee-break table or merchandise table, operating audio or video equipment, managing Wi-Fi infrastructure, or performing other event-related tasks. A person is not a Previous MoneroKon Volunteer because they attended MoneroKon, purchased a ticket, spoke at the event, sponsored the event, donated funds, promoted the event, participated in online discussions, or casually assisted with a task.
- (b) **Contributor** means any person or entity that has provided substantive work, services, materials, or other non-trivial support to the Monero project, whether paid or unpaid, including but not limited to software development, code review, research, cryptographic analysis, protocol design, documentation, translation, infrastructure maintenance, testing. Merely holding, mining, trading, using, donating to, sponsoring, attending events related to, or publicly discussing Monero does not by itself make a person a Contributor.
- (c) The Council maintains a public list of its current members. The Council should ordinarily maintain an odd number of current members.

**2.3 Admission.** A new member is admitted by a special majority (clause 2.7) on the proposal of any existing member.

**2.4 Removal and resignation.** A member or a Fund Signatory may resign at any time by stating so publicly. A member may be removed by a special majority; the same procedure applies to the removal or replacement of a Fund Signatory (clause 4.4).

**2.5 Chair.** The Council elects a Chair from its members by ordinary majority. The Chair convenes meetings, records decisions in the public log (clause 7.4), holds a casting vote on a tie, and gives a first-instance ruling on any question of interpretation, subject to clause 7.2.

**2.6 Meetings and consultation.** Council meetings are held openly in the #monerokon Matrix/IRC room. Any person may attend and speak. The Council should seek the rough consensus of the wider Monero community and should not act against a clear community consensus without giving its reasons.

**2.7 Decisions, quorum and majorities.** A quorum is a majority of current members and in any case not fewer than three.

- (a) An ordinary majority is more than half of those voting; the Chair casts on a tie.
- (b) A special majority is at least two-thirds of all current members. It is required to admit or remove a member; appoint or remove a member of the Organisation Team; appoint, remove, or replace a Fund Signatory; approve a sponsor; and approve expenditure that is major under clause 6.
- (c) Amendment of this Framework requires the higher threshold in clause 7.1.

**2.8 Remuneration and expenses.** Service on the Council is unremunerated. A member may, however, be reimbursed for reasonable expenses properly incurred in the course of that service.

## 3. The Organisation Team

**3.1 Role.** The Organisation Team, also known as the *Orga*, plans and runs the day-to-day operation of each Edition, executing the decisions of the Council and the community.

**3.2 Scope.** The Organisation Team's duties are set out in Schedule 1, which the Council may amend by ordinary majority for each Edition.

**3.3 Appointment.** Members of the Organisation Team are appointed by the Council by special majority for a given Edition. The role may be held by one or more legal or natural persons.

**3.4 Removal and resignation.** Any members of the Organisation Team may resign on reasonable notice. If an Orga member fails to carry out the duties in Schedule 1, the Council may remove them by special majority.

**3.5 Limits.** The Organisation Team may incur and settle expenditure from the operating accounts within a budget approved by the Council. Expenditure that is major under clause 6 requires the Council's prior approval, and any release from the Fund requires the Custodian under clause 4. An Orga member holds no signing authority over the Fund.

**3.6 Remuneration.** The Organisation Team is remunerated for each Edition. The hourly amount for work is determined by the formula in Schedule 2, and is approved by the Council as part of the budget for that Edition; being a major expenditure under clause 6, its approval requires a special majority (clause 2.7(b)). It is released by the Custodian in arrears against periodic invoices under clauses 4.2 and 6.4. A Council member who serves as a member of the Orga takes no part in, and does not vote on, the determination of their own remuneration (clauses 5.1, 5.2).

## 4. Funds Management

**4.1 Role.** The Custodian holds the MoneroKon Fund and signs approved transactions. The Fund is distinct from the operating accounts and payment systems run by the Organisation Team. The Custodian has no authority over the decisions of MoneroKon; its function is custodial only.

**4.2 Release.** The Custodian releases funds where, and only where, (a) the expenditure has been approved by the Council, and (b) the Custodian has received documentation of the amount due, such as an invoice, quote, or receipt, consistent with that approval.

**4.3 Refusal.** The Custodian may decline to sign only a transaction that would be unlawful or would expose a signatory to personal liability. It has no other discretion to withhold an approved disbursement.

**4.4 Signatories and keys.** The Fund is held under control by Fund Signatories. A Fund Signatory is appointed by the Council, and is removed or replaced by the procedure in clause 2.4. The custody arrangement, including how keys are held, distributed, and rotated, follows the [MoneroKon Cryptocurrency Funds Management Policy](https://github.com/MoneroKon/meta/blob/main/policies/funds-management-policy.md).

**4.5 Remuneration and expenses.** Service on the Funds Management group is unremunerated. A member may, however, be reimbursed for reasonable expenses properly incurred in the course of that service.

**4.6 Continuity.** Custody of the Fund and signing authority rest continuously with the Custodian and do not lapse between Editions.

## 5. Separation of duties and conflicts

**5.1 Incompatibility.** A member of the Council may also serve as an Orga member or as a Fund Signatory, but no person may serve as both an an Orga member and a Fund Signatory at the same time. A Council member who serves as an Orga member does not vote on the appointment, removal, scope, budget, or remuneration of the Organisation Team. A Council member who is also a Fund Signatory does not vote on the Council's approval of any disbursement to which they would be a signatory. A member who recuses under this clause remains counted toward the quorum (clause 2.7) but not toward the vote in question.

**5.2 Recusal.** A Council member must declare any personal or financial interest in a matter before the Council, must not vote on it, and may be asked to withdraw from its discussion.

## 6. Spending and approvals

**6.1 Budget and threshold.** The Council approves a budget for each Edition. Expenditure is "major", and requires the Council's prior approval, if it exceeds $5,000 USD or falls outside the approved budget.

**6.2 Reserved categories.** The following are major regardless of amount: venue selection, sponsor approval, and any matter the Council judges to carry significant reputational, legal, or financial risks.

**6.3 Sponsors.** Proposed sponsors are announced in #monerokon for a minimum objection period of one week before the Council confirms them. The Council must consider objections raised within that period. The selection of sponsors should be consistent with [MoneroKon Sponsorship Policy](https://github.com/MoneroKon/meta/blob/main/policies/sponsorship-policy.md).

**6.4 Disbursement.** Approved expenditure is settled by the Organisation Team from the operating accounts within budget, or released by the Custodian from the Fund under clause 4. Recurring approved expenditure is disbursed against periodic invoices presented to the Custodian.

## 7. General provisions

**7.1 Amendment.** This Governance Framework is amended by at least three-quarters of all current Council members.

**7.2 Interpretation and disputes.** A question on the meaning of this Framework is ruled on first by the Chair and may be overridden by the Council by ordinary majority.

**7.3 Continuity.** The Council and the Custodian persist between Editions. The Organisation Team role may be vacant between Editions and appointed afresh for each Edition.

**7.4 Publication.** The membership of the Council, its decisions, and the approved budget for each Edition are published on [MoneroKon website](https://monerokon.org) or Github [MoneroKon Meta repository](https://github.com/MoneroKon/meta).

**7.5 Intellectual property** Website content, written materials, graphics, audio, and video recordings are released under the [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) licence. Under that licence anyone may distribute, remix, adapt, and build upon the materials, including commercially, provided the creator is credited (BY) and any adaptation is released under the same or a compatible licence (SA). The licence grants no rights in the names “Monero Konferenco” and “MoneroKon”, in the MoneroKon logo, or in any associated branding or marks. Those names, the logo, and any associated marks may not be used to name or brand a conference or event, or to suggest endorsement by or affiliation with MoneroKon, except with the Council’s approval by special majority (clause 2.7(b)).

## Schedule 1 — Organisation Team Scope of Work

- **Stakeholder coordination** — coordinate with the venue, suppliers, speakers, sponsors, and volunteers; liaise with speakers on schedules, travel, slides, bios, technical needs, and deadlines; communicate setup instructions and event-day logistics.
- **Sponsors** — manage sponsor deliverables: booths, banners, logos, tickets, speaking slots, and promotional materials.
- **Communications and outreach** — coordinate announcements for tickets, speakers, sponsors, CFPs, workshops, and side events; keep the website and schedule of talks updated; draft press releases, blog posts, and speaker/sponsor announcements; conduct outreach with aligned organisations, communities, and events; host frequent Monero meetups leading up to the event; share weekly operational updates.
- **Ticketing and payments** — maintain the ticketing system; operate the payment systems (BTCPay Server, Stripe, SEPA transfers).
- **Budget and finance** — manage the event budget, tracking costs, vendor quotes, invoices, and receipts; track income, expenses, donations, grants, sponsorships, and ticket revenue; track deadlines for logistics, contracts, payments, and deliverables.
- **Logistics and site** — arrange rental of furniture and equipment: tents, tables, chairs, power, microphones, projectors, screens, and livestreaming support; coordinate teardown and vendor pickup.
- **Volunteers and staff** — recruit, schedule, brief, and supervise volunteers and event staff.
- **Media** — edit and upload presentation videos after the event in a timely manner.
- **Risk and compliance** — take steps to minimise legal, financial, operational, safety, and reputational risks; coordinate with lawyer and accountant as needed.

## Schedule 2 — Organisation Team Remuneration

### 1. Formula

A member of the Organisation Team may claim remuneration (*R*) on an hourly basis for approved work performed for an Edition within the approved budget.

**R = W × F × u × h**

where —

- **(a)** *W* is the applicable benchmark hourly labour rate at the time the budget for the Edition is approved. For a member based in the United States, *W* is the [median hourly wage for “meeting, convention, and event planners”](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Business-and-Financial/Meeting-convention-and-event-planners.htm) most recently published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics in its Occupational Outlook Handbook. For a member based in the European Union, W is the average hourly labour cost in the Member State where the Organiser resides or is incorporated, as most recently reported by [Eurostat in its "Wages and labour costs" statistics⁠](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Wages_and_labour_costs#Labour_costs) report.
- **(b)** *F* is the inflation factor from the wage or labour cost reference month to the Edition's reference month, calculated using CPI-U for a U.S. member and Eurostat HICP for an EU member. *F* equals **1** if the figure is already current to the Edition;
- **(c)** *u* is the contractor uplift, fixed at **1.30**;
- **(d)** *h* is the hours worked.

### 2. Conventions

- **(a) Rounding.** Values are carried unrounded; only the total remuneration *R* is rounded, to the nearest whole dollar or euro.
- **(b) Uplift.** The contractor uplift *u* accounts for self-employment taxes, the absence of employment benefits, paid leave, administrative overhead, and lost opportunity costs.
- **(c) Denomination.** The remuneration is denominated in U.S. dollars or Euros.
- **(d) Conversion to Monero.** Where an invoice is settled in Monero, the amount payable is the dollar or euro amount due divided by the reference rate *X*, then adjusted by the buffer in paragraph (e) —
    - (i) *X* is the Simple Moving Average (50) of the XMR/USD or XMR/EUR price, calculated to the invoice's reference time, being [00:00 UTC] on the invoice date;
    - (ii) *X* is taken from [Investing.com - Monero (XMR) Technical Analysis](https://www.investing.com/crypto/monero/technical), cross-checked against [Trading View - Technical Analysis of Monero (KRAKEN:XMRUSD)](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/XMRUSD/technicals); where the two differ by more than [5]%, their median is used;
    - (iii) the value of *X*, the averaging window, the reference time, and a timestamped capture of each source are recorded in that disbursement's record; and
    - (iv) if a source becomes unavailable or its method changes materially, the Custodian substitutes the equivalent Simple Moving Average (50) from a comparable source and records the substitution.
- **(e) Volatility buffer.** The Monero amount under paragraph (d) is increased by 10% as a reserve against price movement between the reference time and receipt. The Monero paid on an invoice is therefore: **1.10 × (amount due ÷ *X*)**.

### 3. Amendment and confirmation

Changing the structure of the formula, the basis of *R*, or the value of *u* or the buffer is an amendment of this Framework (clause 7.1). Confirming the values of *W*, *F*, *h* for an Edition, and recording *R* at each disbursement, is ordinary business under clause 3.6 and the Custodian's mechanics, and does not require amendment.

### 4. Determination

The values applied for each Edition are recorded in that Edition's budget, evidenced by reference to the relevant BLS and CPI-U publications.
